# Cover Letter Draft — Applied Intelligence

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript entitled:

**“Multi-view Detection of Low-Quality and Synthetic Responses in Login-Free Campus Health Surveys with Portrait Bias Reduction”**

for publication in *Applied Intelligence*.

Campus health surveys are often distributed via share links or QR codes without login. The lack of account anchors exposes response data to random answering, straight-lining, repeated submissions, and increasingly LLM-generated synthetic responses. Beyond classification accuracy, contaminated responses distort **group portrait statistics** that practitioners actually use.

We propose a multi-view detection framework that combines answer-pattern, behavior, and weak batch/propagation signals collected by a deployable survey platform, with late fusion and an optional supervised/semi-supervised variant under sparse human labels. Experiments under controlled contamination evaluate Precision/Recall/F1 and, critically, **reduction of portrait distribution bias** after filtering.

This work fits the journal’s focus on intelligent methods for real-life complex problems. The study follows institutional ethics procedures and uses privacy-preserving hashed device signals with electronic informed consent.

We confirm that the manuscript is original, not under review elsewhere, and that all authors approve submission.
